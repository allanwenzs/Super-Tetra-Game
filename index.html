Privacy Policy for Super Tetra Game

Effective date: 14 March 2026

Super Tetra Game (“the Game”) is developed and published by Nairosoft. This Privacy Policy explains how Super Tetra Game handles information across its available versions, including Android, iOS, and web/PWA builds.

1. Information We Collect

Super Tetra Game is designed to be playable without creating an account or signing in. In normal gameplay, we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. Local Data Stored on Your Device or Browser

Super Tetra Game may store game-related data locally on your device or in your browser, such as:

  • game settings,
  • audio preferences,
  • game progress,
  • scores, achievements, and local gameplay preferences.

This information is stored locally to support gameplay and improve your experience.

3. Advertising

Super Tetra Game may include advertising support in certain versions of the Game, including Android, iOS, and web/PWA builds, and may use third-party advertising services such as Google AdMob where applicable.

These third-party services may collect or process limited device, browser, identifier, and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies in order to deliver, measure, personalise, or improve advertising.

We encourage users to review Google’s privacy information and any relevant third-party advertising policies where applicable.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

If third-party advertising, analytics, hosting, or platform services are used, those services may process limited technical, browser, or device-related information as required for their functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

Super Tetra Game is a puzzle/arcade game and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been provided through the Game, please contact us so we can review the issue.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page, and the updated version will become effective as of the date shown at the top of this policy.
