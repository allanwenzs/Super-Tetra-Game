Privacy Policy for Super Tetra Game

Effective date: 14 March 2026

Super Tetra Game (“the App”) is developed and published by Nairosoft. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Super Tetra Game is designed to be playable without creating an account or signing in. In normal gameplay, we do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

2. Local Data Stored on Your Device

The App may store game-related data locally on your device, such as:

  • game settings,
  • audio preferences,
  • game progress,
  • scores, achievements, and local gameplay preferences.

This information is stored on your device to support gameplay and improve your experience.

3. Advertising

Super Tetra Game includes advertising support in Android builds, including ads provided by third-party services such as Google AdMob.

These third-party services may collect or process certain device and usage data in accordance with their own privacy policies in order to deliver, personalise, and improve advertising.

We encourage users to review Google’s privacy information for advertising services where applicable.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

If third-party advertising or platform services are used, those services may process limited technical or device-related information as required for their functionality.

5. Children’s Privacy

Super Tetra Game is a puzzle/arcade game and is not intended to collect personal information from children knowingly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been provided through the App, please contact us so we can review the issue.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page, and the updated version will become effective as of the date shown at the top of this policy.
